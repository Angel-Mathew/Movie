# Movie Dashboard

A dashboard about the film business, covering revenue, budget, ratings and return on investment by genre, studio and year. It has two pages: Overview and Profitability & Trends.

## The problem
Studios want to know which genres and releases make money. I wanted to compare revenue against budget and rating in one place.

## What you can explore
- Domestic and international revenue, average rating, ROI and number of movies
- Year and genre filters (Action, Adventure, Animation, Comedy, Drama, Family, Fantasy, Musical, Sci-Fi, Thriller)
- Total revenue by studio
- Budget versus revenue
- Average rating by genre

## What I found
Filtering to Animation in 2018 shows two studios, with Ironvale Productions earning 205 in revenue against Wanderlight Films at 131. The same filters can be switched to any genre and year to compare studios.
